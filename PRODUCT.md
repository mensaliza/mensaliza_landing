# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: independent professionals and small businesses in Brazil who collect recurring monthly fees from clients—nutritionists, psychologists, sports coaches, personal trainers, mentors, and subscription-based micro-businesses. They manage 10–100+ assinantes, charge via Pix or bank transfer (not card terminals), and rely heavily on WhatsApp for client communication.

Their context: between sessions, on mobile, juggling client relationships they don't want to strain. The job to be done is predictable monthly revenue without manually chasing each client, while staying in control of who paid and preserving a professional—not collector—relationship.

Secondary audience: existing Mensaliza customers clicking **Entrar** from the landing page to reach the SaaS app.

## Product Purpose

Mensaliza is automated monthly billing for professionals with assinantes. It organizes the subscriber base, sends WhatsApp payment reminders on the correct due date, facilitates Pix payment via a link, and centralizes comprovante approval in a dashboard—without processing payments or holding funds.

Success looks like: visitors who recognize their billing pain schedule a demo; demo attendees convert to paid plans; the landing page clearly communicates that money goes direct to the professional's account and that cobrança doesn't have to feel awkward.

## Positioning

Cobrança mensal automática via WhatsApp que não processa pagamentos—organiza, cobra no dia certo e centraliza comprovantes, enquanto o Pix cai direto na sua conta.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **Agendar demonstração** (free demo, no commitment, setup in minutes)
- Secondary CTA: **Entrar** (link to SaaS app for existing accounts)
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *Pare de cobrar seus clientes um por um. Deixe o Mensaliza fazer isso por você.*
- Belief ladder:
  1. Cobrar mensalidade na mão (WhatsApp + planilha) consome tempo e gera constrangimento.
  2. Existe uma forma automática que não exige maquininha nem intermediário—o dinheiro continua indo direto para mim.
  3. Meus clientes já usam WhatsApp e Pix; não precisam instalar nada novo.
  4. Eu mantenho controle total dos comprovantes e da base de assinantes.
  5. Uma demonstração rápida mostra que funciona para o meu tipo de profissão.
- Proof on hand: copy-complete landing with product mockups (WhatsApp preview, due-date ruler, dashboard visuals). Placeholders pending launch: demo video embed, confirmed plan pricing, app login URL, demo scheduling URL (Calendly/WhatsApp), testimonials or client logos. Collect real proof in `.impeccable/assets/proof/` when available.

## Brand Personality

Voice: direct, empathetic, Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR). Speaks to the emotional weight of cobrar—not just features. Confident without sounding like a debt collector or enterprise fintech.

Three words: **humano**, **organizado**, **aliviador**.

Emotional goal: relief and predictability—*menos peso na consciência, mais previsibilidade no bolso*—plus trust that the professional stays in charge of money and relationships.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing templates: hero metrics, gradient text, cream paper backgrounds, uppercase eyebrow kickers on every section, identical icon-card grids.
- Aggressive collections or debt-recovery tone—Mensaliza removes embarrassment, it doesn't threaten clients.
- Payment-gateway positioning (Stripe/PagSeguro-style "we process your payments")—Mensaliza explicitly does not hold or route funds.
- Over-engineered enterprise billing UI that implies complexity, contracts, or maquininha requirements.
- Visual lanes that feel like every 2026 AI landing: editorial serif drop caps, glassmorphism cards, diagonal stripe backgrounds, or insanely rounded 32px+ card radii.

## Design Principles

1. **Alívio, não cobrança agressiva** — Design and copy should feel like lifting a burden, not adding pressure. The product sends the message; the professional keeps the relationship.
2. **Transparência sobre o dinheiro** — Always make clear: Pix goes direct to the professional, no payment processing, no maquininha. Trust is earned through clarity, not fine print.
3. **Mostrar o fluxo real** — Prefer concrete artifacts (WhatsApp message preview, due-date status, comprovante approval) over abstract feature bullets alone.
4. **Simples para os dois lados** — Parallel clarity for the professional's four steps and the end client's five steps; neither audience should feel they're entering a complex system.
5. **Demo antes de preço fechado** — Pricing is directional until confirmed in demo; CTAs lead to conversation, not self-serve checkout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for the marketing site. Page language is pt-BR (`lang="pt-BR"`). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any scroll or entrance animations. Ensure trust bullets, pricing, and FAQ accordions remain readable at 200% zoom and keyboard-navigable. Status colors (paid/late) must not be the only signal—pair with text labels. Do not use WhatsApp green or any green as a brand or status color.
